By Maria Mendoza, VP, Pay TV Sales, TiVo Consumers today have more ways to watch video than ever before. Streaming services continue to multiply, free and ad-supported options are expanding, and content is available across an unprecedented number of platforms and devices. Despite all of this choice, one challenge persists: finding something to watch.
