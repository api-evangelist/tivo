Sports fans have never had more ways to watch. Fans can choose from live broadcasts, streaming exclusives, regional sports networks, FAST channels, condensed games, replays, highlights and behind-the-scenes content spanning nearly every league and competition in the world. But despite this abundance, one challenge continues to frustrate viewers: finding exactly what they want to watch.
